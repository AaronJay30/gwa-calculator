How to Compute GWA in the Philippines

General Weighted Average (GWA) accounts for the units attached to each subject. Multiply every included grade by its units, add the grade points, then divide by the total included units.

GWA = total grade points ÷ total included units

GWA = Σ(grade × units) ÷ Σ(units)

Calculate your GWA step by step

  1. List each subject's final numerical grade and corresponding units.
  2. Follow your school's rules about which subjects count toward GWA.
  3. Multiply each included grade by its units to get that subject's grade points.
  4. Add the grade points and add the included units separately.
  5. Divide total grade points by total included units.

Worked example

Suppose these two subjects count toward your GWA:

SubjectGradeUnitsGrade × units
Mathematics1.2533.75
Biology1.5046.00
Total—79.75

GWA = 9.75 ÷ 7 = 1.3929

On grading scales where 1.00 is the highest mark, a lower GWA is generally stronger. Check your institution's grading scale.

Do all subjects count toward GWA?

Not always. Rules for PE, NSTP, repeated subjects, incomplete grades, and other courses differ among schools and programs. Include or exclude a course according to your current student handbook or registrar's guidance.

Is a GWA estimate an official Latin Honors result?

No. Latin Honors cutoffs and eligibility requirements vary by institution and may include rules beyond the cumulative average. Check your school's current handbook or ask the registrar before making an academic decision.
